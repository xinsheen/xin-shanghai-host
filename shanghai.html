About Me

I Grew Up Here. Then I Saw the World. Now I Show You the Real Shanghai.

I was born and raised in Shanghai. I've watched this city transform from bicycle lanes to skyscrapers — and I know every quiet corner that survived the change.

For 6 years, I worked as a project manager for a luxury fashion group, overseeing flagship store design projects. That trained my eye for space, light, and atmosphere — but it also taught me what money can't buy: authentic connection.

I've explored nearly every province across China, and lived in Australia for two years. I've backpacked through India, Cambodia, Thailand, Japan, Korea, Germany, and Spain. I know what it's like to land in a foreign country feeling lost — and I know exactly what kind of local friend I wished I'd had.
